Simple Read

Deleting lifting_object broke lift discovery. The robot reliably gets to the cube and closes near it, but the cube never leaves the table. So object_lift_progress is not enough by itself to bootstrap the first lift.

Decision

My recommendation: revert this ablation by restoring lifting_object for the next fresh run, and keep all other rewards unchanged. This is the cleanest next experiment because the ablation isolated the failure to missing lift bootstrap, not bowl transport.

A lower-threshold lift bootstrap reward is also reasonable, but I would not stack that change yet. First restore the term we know previously let lifting emerge.
