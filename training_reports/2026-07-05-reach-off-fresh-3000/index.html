Simple Read

Turning off reach after lift did not break lifting. It may even cleanly reduce reach farming. But it did not solve transport: the cube usually gets lifted high, stays near x≈0.329, and never enters the bowl radius.

ReviewerDeterminationSuggested next move
RL reviewer 1Reach-off learned a cleaner lift-and-hold policy. Lift improved, but transport regressed versus the previous tight-XY continuation.Rebalance post-lift rewards: reduce constant lift incentive and make bowl-XY movement stronger/wider before adding lowering or release.
RL reviewer 2Reach-off was useful for lift acquisition, but not for the object-in-bowl objective. The policy never reached the bowl radius in clean eval.Most isolated next experiment: keep reach-off fixed, but widen OBJECT_TO_BOWL_XY_REWARD_STD from 0.15 back to 0.35.

Decision

The ablation answered the question: stopping reach reward after lift does not hurt lifting, but by itself it does not create bowl transport. The next clean experiment should make post-lift bowl movement easier to learn, preferably by widening the XY reward gradient first while keeping everything else fixed.
