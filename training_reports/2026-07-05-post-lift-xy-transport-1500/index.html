Simple Read

The new reward did what we hoped directionally: once lifted, the cube moves closer to the bowl. But it plateaus around 0.14m away, while success needs inside 0.11m. So the next change should make the XY reward care more about those final centimeters.

What The Reward Paid

Good

The reward gave useful shaping after lift. object_to_bowl_xy reached an eval average of 2.742, and the cube moved closer than baseline.

Problem

With std=0.35, being just outside the bowl still earns a lot of reward. The policy can get paid for “near bowl, still holding high,” without actually entering the bowl radius.
