Decision

More training helped, but not enough to prove stable 90%+ lift. I would treat model_2498.pt as the current best lift checkpoint, then make one small reward/config change if we want to push lift reliability higher.

What improved

The prior report showed about 83.1% lift. This final checkpoint evaluated around 88.5%, so the extra training did move the task forward.

What did not improve enough

The policy briefly crossed 90% in training, but the clean eval stayed below 90%. That means the spike was not a reliable new baseline.

What looks healthy

Reach and close behavior are basically solved in these logs. Drop termination stayed at 0%, so the main blocker is consistency of the actual lift.

What I would not do yet

I would not add bowl transport in the same run if the immediate goal is clean lift reliability. That would mix two questions at once.
