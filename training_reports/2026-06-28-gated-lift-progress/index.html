Decision

Keep the gated lift-progress reward. Next run should probably tighten lift reliability before adding bowl transport.

Why keep it

The previous reach+binary-lift setup often got reach/close but no lift. This run climbed from 0% lift at iteration 100 to 82.6% by the final training batch.

What not to conclude

This does not mean it learned bowl placement. Bowl success stayed 0%, which is expected because this run did not reward transport or release into the bowl.

Why not chase 100%

Getting from 83% to 90-95% is useful. Chasing perfect lift may waste runs or overfit before we test the next skill.

Would 1000 more iterations hit 90%?

Maybe. The curve was still improving late, so another 500-1000 iterations is worth trying. It is not guaranteed because the last part was noisy.
